Deep-work block planner

You don't need 10-hour days. Grad students on Reddit agree that 3–4 hours of real deep work a day is normal and sustainable: put writing in your best hours and do admin and light reading the rest of the day.

Sources: r/AskAcademia deep-work thread (comment consensus); Academia Stack Exchange Q64440 (accepted answer: long days aren't needed, and productivity drops with longer hours).
