Letters of recommendation: what others suggest

If a recommender says they "don't have the bandwidth," treat it as a soft no. Ask directly whether they can write a strong letter, give them a packet of materials, and line up a backup recommender. (Web-search summary of an r/gradadmissions thread; not checked against its comments.)

There's no GPA formula. Programs weigh the whole profile, with research fit and letters weighted heavily, and a weak GPA can be offset by strong recent grades, research experience and letters that address it. (Accepted answer, Academia SE Q38237.)
