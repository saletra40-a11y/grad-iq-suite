What to do with your gown afterward

People search "can you keep your graduation cap and gown" and "what to do with graduation cap and gown." A few good options:

  • Keep it if you'll attend ceremonies as faculty. On Academia Stack Exchange's "Should I buy my PhD cap and gown?", the top answer's author rented at first and later regretted not owning one.
  • Display or preserve it: hang it in a garment bag, or frame the hood.
  • Pass it on to a new grad by selling it.

From the research

Web research found students often rent for their own ceremony and buy later, often generic or used, once they have a faculty job. That comes from search summaries of Reddit threads like "Buy or rent doctoral regalia when graduating?" and "Graduation robe prices are ridiculous", and wasn't checked against their comments.
