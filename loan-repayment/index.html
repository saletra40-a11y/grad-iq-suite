What's changing

  • SAVE borrowers are being given 90 days to choose a new plan (RAP, IBR, ICR, or Standard / Tiered Standard). If you don't choose, you're auto-enrolled in Standard or Tiered Standard.
  • Loans disbursed after July 1, 2026 can only use RAP or Tiered Standard.
  • RAP payments are 1–10% of AGI, with a $10 minimum and forgiveness after 30 years. RAP payments count toward PSLF.

Source: web research file (ETS summary). Reddit's r/StudentLoans advice [search summary, unverified]: watch for your servicer's 90-day notice and choose a plan before you're auto-placed.
