A steadying fact

On Academia Stack Exchange, the accepted answer to "How would one fail a master thesis defense?" says outright failure is rare once your advisor has approved scheduling the defense. A "major revisions" outcome is more typical. And if you hate your dissertation right now, you're in good company (r/GradSchool).
