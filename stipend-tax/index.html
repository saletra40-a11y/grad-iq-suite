Stipend tax worksheet

"Are PhD stipends taxed?" is one of the most common grad-funding questions. Estimate the taxable part of your stipend and set money aside each month so tax time isn't a shock.

Not tax advice. This is a rough planning estimate. Check IRS Publication 970 (Tax Benefits for Education) or a tax professional before you file or make estimated payments.

The rule, in plain words

Per IRS Pub 970, scholarship, fellowship and stipend money is taxable income, except the part you spend on qualified education expenses: tuition and required fees, plus books, supplies and equipment your courses require. Pay for teaching or research work (TA/RA wages) is always taxable.

Source: IRS Publication 970, as summarized in Grad-IQ's web research (Sep 30, 2026).
