Rules as of 2026 — verify with your financial aid office. Caps below come from the ETS summary of the 2026 federal student-loan changes (which links to Federal Student Aid sources). Parts of the new rules are in litigation. Your school sets your cost of attendance and final loan amounts.

The 2026 caps (from July 1, 2026)

  • Grad PLUS is closed to new borrowers.
  • Master's and non-clinical PhD students: $20,500 a year and $100,000 total graduate borrowing.
  • Professional-degree students: $50,000 a year and $200,000 total.
  • $257,500 lifetime cap across all federal student loans (undergrad + grad). Grad PLUS loans count toward it.

Sources: web research file (ETS/FSA summary); the Grad PLUS lifetime-limit point also appears in the Reddit scan (r/GradSchool thread).
