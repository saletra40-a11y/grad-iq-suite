Stress & support · Tool 12

Advisor-meeting log

Write down what you discussed and agreed, then send a short follow-up email. It keeps you both on the same page.

It's also your written record. When advisor conflicts come up, the most common advice from grad students on Reddit is to keep a written record, win over committee members one by one, and if that fails, go to the department head, Director of Graduate Studies or ombudsperson. Dated notes and follow-up emails are that record.

Source: comment consensus on "My advisor won't let me graduate. What do I do?" (r/GradSchool), via Grad-IQ Reddit research.
